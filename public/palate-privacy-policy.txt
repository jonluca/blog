Privacy Policy for Palate

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Palate helps you organize restaurant visits using information on your device. This policy explains local processing, usage analytics, and crash diagnostics.

1. Your restaurant records

Your photos, calendar information, restaurant visit records, and notes are stored and processed on your device. Palate does not require an account or upload your photo library or calendar contents to a Palate server. You control access through iOS permissions and can remove local data by deleting the app. Information you choose to export or share is handled by the destination you select.

2. Usage analytics

Palate uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used. Events include screen views, features used, scan and import counts, export format, and the year selected for summaries. Some restaurant and visit events include an internal record identifier and, when a restaurant is viewed, its name. Firebase can also collect app-instance identifiers and device, operating-system, and app-version information. These events help us improve the app; they are not associated with a Palate account. Photo contents, calendar contents, and personal notes are not included in analytics events.

Firebase Analytics is separate from crash reporting. Google's handling of analytics data is described at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy. Palate does not sell personal information or use these events for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps and websites.

3. Crash and error diagnostics

Production builds send crash and unexpected-error reports to our self-hosted GlitchTip service to help diagnose faults and improve reliability. Reports can include the app version, operating-system version, device model, error type, and technical stack traces.

Crash reports exclude account identifiers, photos, calendar contents, restaurant records, notes, request payloads, screenshots, session recordings, and interaction histories. They are not linked to your identity and are not used for advertising or tracking. Crash reporting is enabled in production builds; it is not an optional in-app setting.

4. Service providers and retention

Google Firebase processes usage analytics. Our hosting provider runs the self-hosted GlitchTip service for crash diagnostics. Apple may separately provide App Store and system diagnostics under its own privacy policy.

Local restaurant data remains on your device until you remove it. Usage analytics and diagnostic reports are stored separately from your local records and used to understand app usage and investigate reliability problems. Contact us with questions about retained data or deletion requests. Because crash reports exclude account identity, we may be unable to identify a particular report as yours.

5. Children

Palate does not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe personal information from a child has been provided so we can investigate.

Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated date.
