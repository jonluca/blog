PasteBin Mobile Privacy Policy

Updated October 25, 2026. This policy describes PasteBin Mobile for iOS, developed by JonLuca DeCaro.

Pastes you submit

When you tap Submit, the app sends your paste text, optional title, syntax selection, visibility setting, and expiration setting to Pastebin.com over HTTPS. The app submits as a guest and does not ask for a Pastebin account, email address, or password. Pastebin.com receives your network address as part of the connection. Anything you include in the text or title is transmitted without removing personal information.

Public pastes can be viewed publicly. Unlisted pastes are accessible to anyone who has the link; they are not private or encrypted. The app requests no automatic expiration. Pastebin.com hosts the submitted content under its own policies and controls its retention and removal. Deleting an entry from the app's local history does not delete the paste from Pastebin.com.

Information on your device

The app stores paste history and returned paste links locally so you can revisit them. This history is not uploaded to our crash-reporting service.

Crash and error reporting

Release versions automatically send technical crash and error reports to our self-hosted GlitchTip service to diagnose failures and improve reliability. Reports include sanitized error categories, stack traces, app versions, and operating-system and device-version information. They exclude paste content, titles, history, URLs, account details, installation identifiers, breadcrumbs, and diagnostic logs. Reports are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking, and are retained for 30 days.

The app has no advertising or usage-analytics SDK. Crash reporting has no in-app opt-out setting in this version. It is separate from the content you explicitly submit to Pastebin.com.

Contact

For questions about this app or its diagnostics, use the developer contact links at jonlu.ca. For a hosted paste, contact Pastebin.com; removing local app data does not remove hosted content.
